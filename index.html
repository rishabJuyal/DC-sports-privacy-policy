Privacy Policy — DC Sports Academy

Effective Date: November 9, 2026  |  App: DC Sports Academy

At DC Sports Academy, we are committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of our students, parents, coaches, and staff. This Privacy Policy outlines how our mobile application collects, uses, protects, and discloses personal information in accordance with applicable data protection laws and Google Play Developer Program Policies.

Core Purpose: The DC Sports Academy application is an operational management and administrative portal designed for offline, in-person sports coaching academies (Cricket, Football, Badminton, and athletic programs). It manages batch rosters, duty attendance, quarterly skill evaluations, and tuition fee ledgers.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information that is strictly necessary for providing academy management and communication services:

  • Personal Identification Information: Student name, date of birth, emergency contact number, parent/guardian name, and email address collected during registration or academy admission.
  • Academy & Training Records: Enrolled sports programs, assigned batches, training schedules, daily attendance check-in records, and coach performance evaluations.
  • Tuition Fee & Payment Records: Monthly or quarterly tuition fee invoice details, offline payment reference numbers (UTR / Transaction IDs), payment timestamps, and payment modes.
    Note: DC Sports Academy does NOT collect or store sensitive financial instruments such as credit card numbers, CVVs, debit card PINs, or net banking passwords.
  • Device & Push Notification Data: Device Push Notification Tokens (via Firebase Cloud Messaging / Expo) strictly utilized to deliver critical operational reminders (e.g., batch cancellations, holiday announcements, and due fee alerts).

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for legitimate academy operations:

  • To process admissions and manage batch capacities.
  • To record student attendance and track training consistency.
  • To generate official tuition fee receipts and maintain transparent ledgers.
  • To deliver critical academy notices, schedule shifts, and emergency updates.
  • To verify coach attendance and compute transparent staff payroll.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted industry-standard infrastructure providers to deliver our services:

  • Supabase (PostgreSQL Database & Auth): Cloud database infrastructure with full encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest, protected by strict Row-Level Security (RLS) policies.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) & Expo: Utilized strictly for delivering real-time push notifications to your mobile device.
  • Razorpay: Trusted payment gateway partner for digital fee payment processing. Payment details are handled securely on PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure.

4. Data Security & Storage

We implement enterprise-grade technical and organizational security measures to protect your data. All communication between the mobile application and backend servers is encrypted using Secure Sockets Layer / Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS). Access to student data is strictly restricted by role-based permissions (Super Admin, Academy Manager, Coach, Parent/Student).

5. Children’s Privacy

While our academy provides sports training to students of various age groups (including minors), the DC Sports Academy mobile application is intended for use by parents, legal guardians, and adult students (aged 18 and above). We do not knowingly solicit or market directly to children under the age of 13. All student admissions and profile information must be created and verified by a parent, legal guardian, or authorized academy administrator.

6. User Rights & Data Deletion Policy

In compliance with Google Play Data Safety policies, users have full rights regarding their personal data:

  • Access & Correction: You may review and update your profile details by contacting the academy desk or your assigned coach.
  • Account & Data Deletion: You can request complete deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time. Simply email us at support@dcsportsacademy.in with your registered phone number or Student ID. Requests are processed within 7 business days.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory updates. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this page.
