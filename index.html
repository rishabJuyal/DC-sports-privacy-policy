Privacy Policy & Data Charter

Application: DC Sports Academy Version: 1.0.0 Effective Date: November 2026

At DC Sports Academy, we maintain a steadfast commitment to the privacy, security, and confidential handling of all student, parent, coach, and staff information. This policy transparently explains our data practices in full compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies.

🏛️

Operational Scope: The DC Sports Academy app is a specialized administrative platform for physical, on-ground sports coaching academies (Martial Arts, Boxing, Cricket, Football, Basketball, Skating, and Athletic Development). It manages student admissions, training schedules, attendance logs, and verified fee receipts.

1. Information We Collect

We strictly limit collection to details essential for sports training and academy management:

👤 Personal Identifiers

Student full name, date of birth, emergency contact telephone number, and parent/guardian email address collected during registration.

🏟️ Academy Rosters

Enrolled sports disciplines, batch allocations, session timings, daily roll-call attendance check-ins, and coach skill ratings.

🧾 Tuition Fee Records

Monthly and quarterly tuition dues, offline reference IDs (UTR numbers), verified payment timestamps, and digital receipts.

🔔 Device Push Tokens

FCM device tokens utilized solely for transmitting urgent batch alerts, holiday announcements, and due fee reminders.

2. How We Utilize Your Information
  • Roster & Capacity Management: To allocate student admissions into age-appropriate sports batches and prevent overcrowded training sessions.
  • Attendance & Safety Accountability: To log daily attendance on the field and ensure emergency contacts are instantly accessible to coaches.
  • Financial Ledgers & Receipts: To record verified tuition settlements, clear outstanding arrears, and issue official downloadable receipts.
  • Academy Operational Alerts: To transmit automated batch timings, coach substitutions, weather notices, and quarterly report publications.
3. Third-Party Infrastructure & Security

All data is maintained on enterprise-grade, certified cloud infrastructure:

  • Supabase (PostgreSQL Cloud Engine): Encrypted in transit via Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) and protected at rest with granular Row-Level Security (RLS) policies.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google FCM): High-reliability notification transport strictly for device-level academy communications.
  • Zero Data Monetization: We do not sell, license, rent, or trade student or family personal information to any marketing or advertisement networks.
4. Children's Privacy & Guardian Governance

While our physical academy coaches children and young athletes, the DC Sports Academy mobile portal is designed for operation by parents, legal guardians, and adult students aged 18 and older. Student registrations and fee authorizations must be completed by a parent, legal guardian, or authorized academy manager.

5. Data Deletion & Account Control

In strict adherence to Google Play Data Safety requirements, every user maintains full autonomy over their stored records:

  • Account Deletion: You may submit an account or data deletion request at any time by emailing rishabjuyal12@gmail.com. All personal identity and profile links will be purged within 7 business days.
  • Profile Rectification: Incorrect contact numbers or names can be rectified directly through your academy branch office or via the in-app profile editor.
